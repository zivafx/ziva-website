Privacy Policy — Gold Market

1. We do not collect personal data

Gold Market does not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal information. The app has:

  • No account and no sign-up. You never enter a name, email address, phone number, or password into the app.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No advertising. This version of the app shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers.
  • No location access. The app never asks for your location and does not use GPS. On first open it reads your phone's region/language setting only, to choose a starting currency. That setting never leaves your device.

2. Data stored only on your device

Some features save information on your phone only, inside the app's own storage:

  • My Gold entries (item names, weights, karats and buy prices that you type in)
  • Your chosen currency, language, price alert and notification time
  • A cached copy of the most recent prices, so the app can show the last known rates

This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted with it. You can also clear it at any time from your phone's app settings.

3. Network requests — prices only

To show fresh prices, the app connects to the following public price services:

  • gold-api.com — live gold, silver, platinum and palladium spot prices
  • open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API) — currency exchange rates
  • api.nbp.pl (National Bank of Poland) — public historical gold prices used by the price chart

These requests ask only for price data. They contain no personal information, no device identifiers created by the app, and nothing from your My Gold entries. As with any internet request, the receiving service can see a standard technical request (such as your IP address) — that is handled under that service's own privacy policy.

4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily price notification, it is scheduled and shown on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to any server.

5. Sharing rates

If you tap a "share" button, the rates text is shared through the app you choose (for example a messaging app) using Android's standard share sheet. We receive nothing from this.

6. Children

Gold Market is a finance information app intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above (for example, if ads are added), this policy will be updated first, the effective date above will change, and the app's Play Store data-safety section will be updated to match.
