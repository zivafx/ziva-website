ZIVABacktesting

A free backtesting app for gold & forex traders — practise on real historical price data with a full replay system, TradingView-style drawing tools and a built-in trade journal.

Download for Android (Free) View on GitHub

Everything you need to practise properly

A complete backtesting workspace in your pocket — built for traders who practise before they risk real money.

Historical Replay

Replay real historical price action bar by bar on 9 instruments — gold, silver and 7 major forex pairs. All data is stored inside the app, so it works fully offline.

Replay Controls

Play and pause the market, step forward candle by candle, and choose your speed from 1× to 10×. Switch between multiple replay timeframes to match the way you trade.

Drawing Tools

TradingView-style tools: trend lines, rays, rectangles and zones with labels, Fibonacci retracement, circles, paths and text — with magnet snapping and reusable drawing templates for your setups.

Trade Journal & Analytics

Log every practice trade and see the truth in numbers: a dashboard with win rate, profit/loss tracking and session statistics that show whether your strategy is actually improving.

Built-in Notebook

Keep your trading rules, plans and lessons inside the app — your notebook sits next to your charts, sessions and journal, so your process stays in one place.

Dark & Light Themes

A pure black-and-gold dark theme for night sessions, and a clean light theme for the day. Pick the one your eyes prefer — the chart, tools and journal all follow it.

Free forever — no account, no paywall, and your data stays on your phone.

About ZivaFX

ZivaFX is an independent software project from Lahore, Pakistan, building free tools for traders. Ziva Backtesting was made so that any trader — beginner or experienced — can practise on real market history, test ideas honestly and keep a proper journal, without paying for expensive software.
