Delete Your Account — Ziva Backtesting

App: Ziva Backtesting (package: com.zivaapps.zivabacktesting)
Developer: ZivaApps
Contact: contact@zivaapps.online

This page explains how to delete your Ziva Backtesting account and the data associated with it. Deletion is free and can be done at any time.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Ziva Backtesting and sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when asked. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to contact@zivaapps.online from the same email address as the Google account you use in the app.
  2. Write "Delete my account" in the subject or the message.
  3. We verify the request came from the account's own email address and delete the account. Requests are completed within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes your sign-in account record held for Ziva Backtesting through Firebase Authentication, which contains only:

  • your name (as provided by your Google account),
  • your email address,
  • your account user ID.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered, and the same account can only be used again by signing in fresh, which creates a new account.

What data is kept

None. ZivaApps keeps no copy of your account data after deletion, and there is no additional retention period. We do not hold any other personal data about you: your practice sessions, trades, notes and drawings are stored only on your own device — they are never uploaded to us — and you can remove them at any time from inside the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Guests

If you only used the app in guest mode and never signed in, no account and no personal data exist, so there is nothing to delete.
