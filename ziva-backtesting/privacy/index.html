Privacy Policy — Ziva Backtesting

App: Ziva Backtesting (package com.zivaapps.zivabacktesting)
Developer: ZivaApps
Contact: contact@zivaapps.online
Website: https://zivaapps.online
Effective date: 16 November 2026

1. Guest mode collects nothing; accounts are optional

Ziva Backtesting opens in guest mode. As a guest you can look around the app freely, and nothing about you is collected — no name, no email, nothing.

If you want to do real work in the app (create sessions, use the drawing tools, place practice trades, save notes), the app asks you to sign in with Google. Signing in is handled by Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication. Through it we receive only the basic account details needed to run your account:

  • your name and email address, and your Google account identifier

This information is used only to identify your account inside the app. It is never sold, never shared with third parties for marketing, and never used for advertising. We do not receive or store your Google password.

The app also has:

  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No advertising in this version. This version of the app shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers.
  • No location access. The app never asks for your location and does not use GPS.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your practice work is saved on your phone only, inside the app's own storage:

  • Your backtesting sessions (symbol, dates, starting balance, replay position, practice trades and their results)
  • Your notebook journal entries
  • Your app settings (for example theme and favourite timeframes)
  • A cached copy of downloaded market history, so charts open faster

This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — signing in unlocks the work features, it does not upload your sessions, trades or notes anywhere. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted with it. You can also clear it at any time from your phone's app settings.

3. Deleting your account

If you created an account by signing in, you can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete Account — this removes your account and the sign-in information held for it.
  • Without the app: email contact@zivaapps.online from the email address of the account and ask for deletion; we will delete the account and confirm by reply.

Work stored on your own device (sessions, notes) is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage, as described above.

4. Network requests — market data only

To show live prices and to update market history, the app connects to public market-data services, including:

  • gold-api.com — live gold, silver, platinum and palladium prices
  • data-api.binance.vision (Binance public data) — crypto prices and history
  • open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API), api.frankfurter.dev and public currency-rate mirrors — exchange rates used to build cross pairs
  • api.nbp.pl (National Bank of Poland) — public historical gold prices
  • api.exchange.coinbase.com — crypto market data

These requests ask only for market price and history data. They contain no personal information, no device identifiers created by the app, and nothing from your sessions or notebook. As with any internet request, the receiving service can see a standard technical request (such as your IP address) — that is handled under that service's own privacy policy.

5. Virtual trades only

All trades inside Ziva Backtesting are virtual practice trades on historical data. The app has no brokerage connection, takes no deposits, and moves no real money. Nothing you do in the app can create a real financial transaction.

6. Children

Ziva Backtesting is a trading-practice and education app intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above (for example, if ads are added, or accounts gain cloud sync), this policy will be updated first, the effective date above will change, and the app's Play Store data-safety section will be updated to match.
